# ASTRA Knowledge — Astrology: Ancient to Present
domain: astrology
last_updated: 2026-05-06

## What Is Astrology

Astrology is the study of the relationship between celestial bodies and terrestrial events, human character, and cycles of time. It is not prediction in the fatalistic sense — it is a symbolic language mapping cosmic cycles onto human experience. The premise: the heavens and earth reflect one another (as above, so below — the Hermetic axiom from the Emerald Tablet). Across every major civilization from Sumer to China to Mesoamerica, humans independently developed astrological systems. This convergence is itself significant.

## Mesopotamian and Babylonian Origins

The oldest systematic astrology originates in Babylon (c. 2000 BCE). The MUL.APIN tablet series (c. 1000 BCE) catalogs stars, planets, and their omens. Babylonian astrology was primarily mundane — predicting events for kings and nations, not individuals. The seven classical planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) were identified and their cycles tracked with extraordinary precision. The Saros cycle (18 years, 11 days) for eclipse prediction was known by 700 BCE. The zodiac of 12 signs was codified by approximately 500 BCE.

Key texts: Enuma Anu Enlil (70 tablets of celestial omens), MUL.APIN, the Astronomical Diaries (daily planetary observations from 652 BCE onward).

## Hellenistic Astrology (c. 300 BCE – 600 CE)

The synthesis of Babylonian observation with Greek philosophy produced the most technically sophisticated premodern astrological system. Key developments: the horoscope (natal chart cast for birth moment), house systems, aspects (angular relationships between planets), dignities (domicile, exaltation, triplicity, term, face), lots (Arabic parts), and sect (day/night charts).

Primary texts: Tetrabiblos (Ptolemy, c. 150 CE) — the foundational systematic text. Mathesis (Firmicus Maternus, c. 334 CE). Astronomica (Manilius, c. 10 CE). Anthology (Vettius Valens, c. 175 CE) — most comprehensive surviving practical manual.

The Ascendant (rising sign) and its lord govern the life force. The sect light (Sun by day, Moon by night) governs vitality. The Lot of Fortune maps material circumstances. This system was highly deterministic but nuanced — malefics (Mars, Saturn) and benefics (Jupiter, Venus) modified by sect, dignity, and aspect.

## Essential Dignities

Each planet has five forms of dignity: Domicile (own sign — strongest), Exaltation (sign of greatest power), Triplicity (element ruler by day/night), Term (5-degree rulership), Face (10-degree decan rulership). A planet in its domicile or exaltation acts with full power. A planet in detriment (opposite domicile) or fall (opposite exaltation) is weakened.

Domiciles: Sun=Leo, Moon=Cancer, Mercury=Gemini/Virgo, Venus=Taurus/Libra, Mars=Aries/Scorpio, Jupiter=Sagittarius/Pisces, Saturn=Capricorn/Aquarius. Outer planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) assigned modern co-rulerships.

Exaltations: Sun=Aries, Moon=Taurus, Mercury=Virgo, Venus=Pisces, Mars=Capricorn, Jupiter=Cancer, Saturn=Libra, North Node=Gemini.

## Major Aspects

Conjunction (0°) — fusion, intensity, the blending of two energies. Strongest aspect.
Opposition (180°) — polarity, tension, awareness through relationship and projection.
Trine (120°) — harmony, ease, natural talent, flowing energy between signs of the same element.
Square (90°) — friction, challenge, the dynamic tension that produces growth and action.
Sextile (60°) — opportunity, support, cooperative energy requiring activation.
Quincunx/Inconjunct (150°) — adjustment, dis-ease, two planets with no common ground requiring constant recalibration.
Semi-sextile (30°) — mild opportunity, subtle connection.
Quintile (72°) — creative talent, gifts, the 5th harmonic.

Orbs vary by tradition: Hellenistic used wide orbs (up to 15° for luminaries). Modern practice generally uses 8° for major aspects, tighter for outer planets.

## Vedic Astrology (Jyotish)

The system of light (Jyotish = science of light). Uses the sidereal zodiac (fixed stars) rather than the tropical zodiac (seasons). The difference between them — the ayanamsha — is approximately 23-24 degrees currently (the Lahiri ayanamsha is the standard). This means a tropical Aries Sun is often a sidereal Pisces Sun.

Key differences from Western: whole sign houses (standard), Ascendant lord is paramount, Nakshatras (27 lunar mansions) are central, no outer planets in classical tradition (Rahu/Ketu = Nodes are used instead of Neptune/Uranus/Pluto), Dashas (planetary periods) provide timing.

The 9 Grahas: Sun (Surya), Moon (Chandra), Mars (Mangala), Mercury (Budha), Jupiter (Guru/Brihaspati), Venus (Shukra), Saturn (Shani), Rahu (North Node), Ketu (South Node).

Nakshatras: 27 lunar mansions of 13°20' each, corresponding to the Moon's daily movement. Each Nakshatra has a ruling planet (Navagraha), a deity, a symbol, and a quality (Deva/Manushya/Rakshasa — divine/human/demonic).

Vimshottari Dasha: 120-year planetary period cycle. Sequence: Sun(6), Moon(10), Mars(7), Rahu(18), Jupiter(16), Saturn(19), Mercury(17), Ketu(7), Venus(20). The dasha lord and its condition in the natal chart governs the quality of that life period. Sub-periods (Bhuktis) refine timing.

Yogas: Specific planetary combinations producing defined results. Raja Yoga (kingly success) — lords of trines and angles in conjunction. Dhana Yoga (wealth) — lords of 2nd and 11th in strong condition. Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga — a debilitated planet's cancellation producing extraordinary rise. Kemadruma Yoga — Moon without adjacent planets, producing isolation.

## Chinese Astrology

Based on a 60-year cycle combining 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches (animals). The 12 animals: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. Five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) cycle through each animal year. Ba Zi (Four Pillars of Destiny) uses year, month, day, and hour pillars — each with a stem and branch — to map a complete cosmic blueprint. The Day Master (day stem) is the self. Favorable and unfavorable elements determine life quality. 

Purple Star Astrology (Zi Wei Dou Shu) — a complex palace-based system using 14 major stars distributed across 12 life palaces. Considered the most sophisticated form of Chinese astrology.

## Mayan Astrology and the Tzolkin

The Tzolkin: a 260-day sacred calendar combining 20 day signs with 13 numbers. The combination produces 260 unique day-signs, each with specific qualities. Used for divination, naming children, determining fate, and ritual timing. The 20 day signs: Imix (crocodile), Ik (wind), Akbal (night), Kan (seed), Chicchan (serpent), Cimi (death), Manik (deer), Lamat (star), Muluc (water), Oc (dog), Chuen (monkey), Eb (road), Ben (reed), Ix (jaguar), Men (eagle), Cib (owl/vulture), Caban (earth), Etznab (flint), Cauac (storm), Ahau (sun/lord).

The Haab (365-day solar calendar) interlocks with the Tzolkin to produce the Calendar Round of 52 years. The Long Count tracks larger cycles — the famous 13-baktun cycle ending December 21, 2012 marked the completion of a 5,125-year world age.

## Horary Astrology

A chart cast for the exact moment a question is sincerely asked. The heavens at that moment answer the question. Technique: identify the querent (Ascendant and its lord), the quesited (house governing the matter), and whether significators apply to each other by aspect. If they do, the matter completes. Reception (one planet in the dignity of the other) indicates desire and willingness. Prohibitions (a third planet intercepts before the aspect perfects) indicate obstacles.

Primary authority: William Lilly, Christian Astrology (1647) — the definitive English horary text. Still used verbatim by traditional practitioners.

## Mundane Astrology

The astrology of nations, collective events, weather, and history. Ingress charts (Sun entering cardinal signs — especially Aries ingress) set the tone for the season/year. Eclipse cycles (Saros series) correlate with political upheaval. Jupiter-Saturn conjunctions (the great conjunctions, ~20-year cycle) mark generational shifts. Saturn-Pluto conjunctions correlate with structural collapse and rebuilding. Uranus-Pluto squares produce revolutionary tension (1965-66 and 2012-2015). The natal chart of a nation (its founding moment) is transited and progressed like an individual chart.

## Secondary Progressions and Transits

Secondary Progressions: each day after birth = one year of life (day-for-a-year). The progressed Sun moves approximately 1° per year — a complete sign in ~30 years. Progressed New Moon marks a new 30-year cycle. Progressed Moon (moving ~1° per month in real time = 1 month per year in progressed time) acts as a timer.

Solar Arc Directions: all planets moved forward at the rate of the progressed Sun (~1° per year). Major events correlate with solar arc planets hitting natal angles or sensitive points.

Transits: actual current planetary positions aspecting natal planets. Saturn transits (every 7 years to each natal planet) bring structure, testing, and maturation. Jupiter transits expand and open doors. Pluto transits transform irreversibly. The Saturn Return (age 29-30 and 58-60) is the most universally recognized life restructuring point.

## Electional Astrology

Choosing an auspicious moment to begin an enterprise. The Ascendant represents the matter being initiated — it must be strong, unafflicted, and its lord powerful. The Moon should be waxing (for growth) and applying to a benefic. Malefics should not be on angles. The 8th house should be avoided for most matters. Used historically for coronations, business launches, marriages, military campaigns, and building foundations.

## The Hellenistic Lots (Arabic Parts)

Mathematically derived sensitive points. The Lot of Fortune: Ascendant + Moon - Sun (day chart) / Ascendant + Sun - Moon (night chart). Maps material fortune and the body. The Lot of Spirit: Ascendant + Sun - Moon (day) / Ascendant + Moon - Sun (night). Maps the soul, agency, and career. The Lot of Eros, Necessity, Courage, Victory, Nemesis complete the seven hermetic lots.

## Astrological Ages

The precession of equinoxes moves backward through the zodiac at ~1° per 72 years — a complete cycle in ~25,920 years (the Platonic Great Year). Each sign takes ~2,160 years (an astrological age). Currently transitioning from Pisces to Aquarius. The Age of Pisces (~0 CE to ~2150 CE): monotheistic religion, sacrifice, the fish symbol, dissolution of the old world. The Age of Aquarius: collective consciousness, technology, brotherhood, the dissolution of hierarchy. The boundary is disputed — some place it in 1962 (when Uranus and Pluto conjoined in early Virgo opposite a Pisces stellium), others in 2012, others not until 2597.

## Fixed Stars

Major fixed stars used in traditional astrology. Regulus (29° Leo traditionally, now 0° Virgo due to precession) — the heart of the Lion, royalty, success with risk of downfall. Algol (26° Taurus) — the most malefic star, the head of Medusa, associated with violence and loss. Spica (23° Libra) — the spike of wheat held by Virgo, the most benefic star, gifts of genius. Antares (9° Sagittarius) — heart of the Scorpion, rival of Mars, military, destruction and honor. Aldebaran (9° Gemini) — eye of the Bull, integrity, success. Fomalhaut (3° Pisces) — one of the four royal stars (Watchers of Heaven), spiritual success. Sirius (13° Cancer) — the brightest star, the Dog Star, sacred to Egypt (Isis), fame and wealth.

## Initiatic and Esoteric Astrology

Alice Bailey's Esoteric Astrology (1951) — soul-centered astrology using esoteric rulers. The esoteric ruler of Aries is Mercury (not Mars), of Taurus is Vulcan, of Gemini is Venus, of Cancer is Neptune, of Leo is the Sun (same as exoteric), of Virgo is the Moon (veiling Neptune), of Libra is Uranus, of Scorpio is Mars (same), of Sagittarius is the Earth, of Capricorn is Saturn (same), of Aquarius is Jupiter, of Pisces is Pluto.

The Theosophical tradition (Blavatsky, Besant) mapped the seven rays onto planets. The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn assigned each planet a sephirah on the Tree of Life: Saturn=Binah, Jupiter=Chesed, Mars=Geburah, Sun=Tiphareth, Venus=Netzach, Mercury=Hod, Moon=Yesod, Earth=Malkuth. The outer planets: Uranus=Chokmah, Neptune=Kether, Pluto=Da'ath.

Dane Rudhyar's Humanistic/Transpersonal Astrology — the chart as a mandala of the self, astrology as psychological tool rather than deterministic fate. Influenced Jung.
