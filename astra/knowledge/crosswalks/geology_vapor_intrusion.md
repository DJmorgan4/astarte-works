# Crosswalk: geology ↔ vapor_intrusion

> Retrieve for any VI screen. Geology sets the transport medium; VI
> reasoning is only as good as the stratigraphic model under it.

## SIGNAL FLOW
geology → vapor_intrusion:
- Stratigraphy and depth to water → vertical separation available for
  PVI biodegradation shield vs. CVI (no shield).
- Fracture networks in clay/marl → bypass the assumed clean-soil barrier;
  a "protective" 15 ft of clay riddled with desiccation fractures is not
  protective.
- Permeable lenses / sand seams → lateral vapor highways beyond plume edge.
- Capillary breaks and moisture profile → diffusion vs. advection balance.

## COMBINED RED FLAGS
- Chlorinated source + fractured Eagle Ford/Taylor residuum → CVI screening
  distances understate risk; fractures defeat matrix diffusion assumptions.
- Shallow permeable lens connecting plume to building footprint.
- Utility/sewer bedding gravel in low-perm native soil → engineered conduit
  the soil column would otherwise have blocked.
- Petroleum source but large paved footprint over low-perm soil → oxygen
  shadow suppresses the aerobic shield PVI logic relies on.
