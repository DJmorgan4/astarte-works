# Crosswalk: stormwater ↔ soils

> Retrieve for BMP selection, basin sizing, and stabilization planning.
> Soils physics dictates which controls actually work; most field BMP
> failures are soil-mismatch failures.

## SIGNAL FLOW
soils → stormwater:
- Hydrologic Soil Group (A–D) → runoff volume, infiltration BMP viability
  (infiltration is a non-starter on HSG D clays).
- K-factor (erodibility) → erosion control aggressiveness.
- Texture / shrink-swell → slope stabilization method and seeding success.
- Drainage class + depth to water → basin feasibility, dewatering.

## COMBINED RED FLAGS
- Silt fence specified on fat clay (Houston Black/Heiden) with concentrated
  flow → wrong BMP for both flow type and soil; predictable blowout.
- Hydromulch alone on >3:1 vertisol cut slopes → underperforms; needs
  blankets + timed perennial establishment.
- Infiltration BMP proposed on HSG D → will not infiltrate; becomes a pond.
- Winter/summer disturbance outside DFW spring/fall seeding windows → bare
  clay exposed through the erosive season.

## EXAMPLE QUERIES
- "12-acre site on Houston Black, 4:1 cut slopes — erosion control train?"
- "Can I use an infiltration basin here, or is the soil group against me?"
